The Property

The right home on the right lot.

Rarely does the right home on the right lot become available in Latitude Margaritaville Hilton Head — and this one truly has it all. Perfectly positioned on a premium lagoon-to — woods homesite, this beautifully upgraded Coconut model offers exceptional privacy, tranquil views, and an expansive outdoor living space highlighted by a huge paver patio ideal for entertaining.

Inside, the home features 1,684 sq ft of thoughtfully designed living space with 2 bedrooms, 2 full baths, plus a den/office. Upgraded laminate flooring runs throughout the home with tile baths and absolutely no carpet anywhere. The chef's kitchen showcases top-of — the-line Kitchen Aid appliances, gas range, upgraded cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and quartz countertops.

Custom upgrades continue with elegant moldings, exposed wood beams in the great room, and a coffered ceiling in the primary bedroom. The spa — inspired primary bath includes an oversized tiled shower with rain head. Outdoor living shines with a large screened lanai and expansive paver patio — both overlooking the peaceful lagoon and private forest backdrop.

A spacious two — car garage completes this exceptional home, designed for comfort, style, and effortless entertaining.
