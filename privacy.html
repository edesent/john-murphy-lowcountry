Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. When you submit the contact form or the free home valuation form, we receive the information you type into it — typically your name, email address, phone number, the property address or your goal, and any message you write.

This website does not use advertising cookies and does not build a profile of you. Our host, Vercel, records anonymous, aggregated page-view statistics that do not identify individual visitors.

How we use it

We use your information for one purpose: to respond to you about buying, selling, or relocating in the South Carolina Lowcountry. If you ask for a home valuation, we use the property details you give us to prepare it.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not add you to a marketing list without your consent, and we do not share your details with third-party advertisers.

Email and text messages

By submitting a form you are asking us to contact you, and you consent to us replying by email, phone, or text message using the details you provided. Message and data rates may apply to text messages. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying STOP to a text, or by emailing or calling us using the details below — we will honour that immediately.

Who else handles your information

Two services are involved in operating this site:

  • ✓ FormSubmit — delivers form submissions to our email inbox. Your submission passes through their service on the way to us.
  • ✓ Vercel — hosts this website and records anonymous traffic statistics.

Neither is authorised to use your information for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our email records for as long as we may reasonably need them to serve you. You may ask us to delete your information at any time and we will do so, unless a real estate transaction record must be retained under South Carolina law.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us and we will respond.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
