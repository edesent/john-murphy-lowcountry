The Property

Waterfront luxury in Latitude Lakes.

Waterfront Luxury in Latitude Lakes! Welcome to 165 Topside E in Hardeeville, SC, a custom — built waterfront retreat located in the highly desirable Latitude Lakes, a non — age-restricted enclave within Latitude Margaritaville. Offering over 2,500 square feet of beautifully designed living space, this 3 — bedroom, 2.5 — bath home blends custom craftsmanship, relaxed Lowcountry elegance, and the unmatched lifestyle of lakeside living.

Step inside to an open — concept floor plan designed for both everyday comfort and effortless entertaining. The heart of the home is the impressive chef's kitchen, complete with high — end finishes, an induction range, generous prep space, and seamless flow into the main living area. A beautiful fireplace with custom built — ins adds warmth, style, and a true sense of home.

Thoughtfully designed with luxury finishes throughout, this residence delivers the feel of a private lakeside sanctuary while still enjoying the energy, amenities, and lifestyle of Latitude Margaritaville. One of the home's standout features is the huge waterfront heated and cooled lanai, creating a true indoor/outdoor living experience that can be enjoyed comfortably year — round.

Outside, the home continues to impress with a private dock and paver patio, offering the perfect setting for morning coffee by the water, sunset cocktails, entertaining friends, or enjoying life on the lake with your own boat at the dock. Whether you are drawn to boating, peaceful water views, outdoor living, or the convenience of resort — style amenities nearby, this home offers a rare combination: custom waterfront living inside one of the Lowcountry's most exciting lifestyle communities.165 Topside E is more than a home - it is a lakeside lifestyle.
