The Property

Move-in ready — why wait to build?

WHY WAIT TO BUILD? This move-in — ready Toll Brothers Saltmeadow offers nearly 2,800 sq. ft. of beautifully designed single — level living, a 3 — car garage, and a private wooded homesite in Riverton Pointe. Completed in 2025 and lightly lived in, this 3 — bedroom, 2.5 — bath home delivers the look and feel of new construction without the construction wait, along with the added peace of mind of a transferable builder warranty.

Inside, the open — concept design was created for both everyday comfort and entertaining. The chef — inspired kitchen features gas cooking, abundant cabinetry, pantry storage and a spacious gathering area that flows naturally into the dining and great room. Engineered hardwood flooring, a fireplace, tray ceilings and thoughtful architectural details add warmth and character throughout.

The main — level primary suite provides a relaxing retreat with multiple closets and a beautifully appointed bath. Two additional bedrooms offer flexibility for family and guests, while the separate office/library/den creates valuable space for working from home, hobbies or a quiet retreat. Step outside to the screened porch and enjoy the privacy and tranquility of the wooded backyard-a welcome contrast to many homes still surrounded by active construction.

A spacious 3 — car garage, tankless water heater, smart thermostat and numerous Toll Brothers upgrades complete the package. Located within gated Riverton Pointe Golf & Country Club, residents enjoy an exceptional Lowcountry lifestyle centered around the Jack Nicklaus — designed golf course, resort — style pool, clubhouse, fitness center, tennis, pickleball, dining and an active social calendar.

Move-in ready. Private wooded setting. Nearly 2,800 sq. ft. Three — car garage. Toll Brothers quality — without the wait. Come experience 1628 Laurel Oak Avenue and discover why this may be one of Riverton Pointe's best opportunities.
