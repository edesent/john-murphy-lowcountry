The Property

Golf, water, and a private resort backyard.

Why settle for ordinary when you can have golf, water, privacy, and a private resort — style backyard all in one exceptional Riverton Pointe home? Welcome to 246 Laurel Oak Avenue, a beautifully upgraded Toll Brothers residence set on one of the most desirable view corridors in the community. Perfectly positioned with sweeping views of the 6th and 7th fairways, plus a peaceful lagoon backdrop, this home delivers the kind of Lowcountry setting buyers wait for — but without the wait of new construction.

Built in 2023, this 2,986 sq. ft. home offers 3 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, a versatile loft, private office/flex space, and a 3 — car garage. The open — concept layout is bright, comfortable, and designed for easy everyday living as well as effortless entertaining. The great room flows beautifully into the dining area and gourmet kitchen, where upgraded appliances, a 5 — burner gas cooktop, wall oven, generous pantry space, and quality finishes create a polished yet welcoming centerpiece for the home.

The main — level primary suite provides a private retreat with thoughtful design, spacious closets, and a spa — inspired bath. Upstairs, the loft adds valuable bonus living space — perfect for guests, hobbies, media, fitness, or a second lounge area. Step outside and the home truly separates itself from the competition. The custom in — ground pool, covered/enclosed porch, outdoor grill area, fenced yard, and long golf — and-lagoon views create a backyard experience that feels more like a private club escape than a typical homesite.

Morning coffee, sunset cocktails, weekend guests, or quiet afternoons by the pool — this is the lifestyle buyers come to Riverton Pointe to find. Located in Riverton Pointe Golf & Country Club, a staff — gated Toll Brothers community featuring an acclaimed Jack Nicklaus — designed golf course, resort — style amenities, dining, fitness, pickleball, tennis, and a vibrant social setting, this home offers the perfect blend of luxury, recreation, and Lowcountry ease.

Newer construction. Premium views. Private pool. 3 — car garage. Finished outdoor living. This is not just another home in Riverton Pointe — this is the one that already has the lifestyle built in.
