Relocation & Retirement

Mild winters, no state tax on Social Security, generally more home for your money compared to the Northeast and Midwest, and an amenity-rich, active lifestyle built around golf, boating, and beaches are the top reasons buyers give John.

South Carolina does not tax Social Security benefits and offers deductions on other forms of retirement income. This is general guidance only — please consult a licensed CPA for advice specific to your situation.

John and Deb coordinate the timing of your current home sale with your Lowcountry purchase, helping sequence financing, closings, and moving logistics so you're never carrying two homes longer than necessary. See our full Relocation Guide.

A retirement specialist focuses on the specific needs of retiring buyers — proximity to healthcare, community amenities, HOA structures in 55+ communities, and the realities of downsizing or relocating later in life.

Communities

Bluffton offers historic charm, new construction, and the most master-planned communities. Hilton Head Island offers beach access, resort-style amenities, and championship golf. Beaufort offers antebellum architecture and small-town character on a working waterfront. See our full Communities Guide.

Sun City Hilton Head is one of the largest active-adult (55+) communities on the East Coast, located in Okatie, SC, featuring multiple golf courses, several clubhouses, and an extensive calendar of clubs and social activities.

Latitude Margaritaville is a 55+ active-adult community in Hardeeville, SC built around the Jimmy Buffett "island lifestyle" brand, with a bandshell, resort-style pools, and a laid-back, social culture.
