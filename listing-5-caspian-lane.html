The Property

A fresh opportunity in Oldfield.

Experience the Oldfield lifestyle without sacrificing the space, flexibility and everyday comfort today's buyers are looking for. Offering 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths and more than 2,500 square feet, 5 Caspian Lane combines classic Lowcountry character with an easy, functional floor plan designed for the way people live today.

A large front porch overlooking the pond creates an inviting first impression and the perfect spot for morning coffee or an evening glass of wine. Inside, recently updated luxury vinyl plank flooring enhances the first floor, where an open great room with fireplace flows naturally into the kitchen and dining areas — comfortable for everyday living yet ready for entertaining.

The first-floor primary suite provides privacy and convenience, while three additional bedrooms and flexible upstairs space give you options for guests, a home office, media room or hobby space. A screened porch extends the living area outdoors, while the detached two-car garage provides additional storage and functionality.

What truly sets this home apart is everything that comes with living in Oldfield. Enjoy the Greg Norman Signature golf course, the Okatie River and Outfitters Center, boating and fishing, equestrian facilities, pools, fitness and racquet sports, walking trails, dining at the River Club and Magnolia Grill, and a year-round calendar of activities.5 Caspian Lane isn't simply a home in Oldfield — it's an opportunity to enjoy one of the Lowcountry's most distinctive lifestyles in a home that offers the space and versatility buyers want most.

Come experience it for yourself.
